BATTERY KNOWLEDGE

UNDERSTAND YOUR BATTERY.

01

What does Ah mean?

Ah means ampere-hour. It indicates a battery's capacity to deliver current over time. Higher Ah generally means more stored capacity, when comparing batteries of the same type and conditions.

02

What is CCA?

CCA means Cold Cranking Amps. It indicates how much starting current an automotive battery can provide in cold conditions. A suitable CCA rating helps the vehicle start reliably.

03

Tubular vs other batteries

Tubular batteries are a common deep-cycle option for inverter backup systems and are designed for repeated charge-discharge use. The right type depends on backup needs, usage pattern, space and budget.

04

When should you replace a battery?

Common signs include slow engine cranking, repeated starting problems, swelling or leakage, reduced backup time, or a battery that no longer holds charge properly. Have it tested before replacing it.
